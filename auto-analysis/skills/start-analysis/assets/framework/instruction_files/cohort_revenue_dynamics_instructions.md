# Cohort Revenue Dynamics Analysis Framework
*Discovery-Driven Revenue Pattern Understanding*

---

## 1 · Objective

Discover and understand **customer revenue evolution patterns** through hypothesis-driven cohort analysis. This framework identifies how different customer groups generate revenue over time, providing foundational insights for business strategy and advanced modeling.

### Core Principles:
- **Discovery-Driven:** Let data and business context reveal relevant cohort dimensions and patterns
- **Hypothesis-Tested:** Validate patterns through systematic hypothesis testing
- **Business-Interpretable:** Translate findings into clear, actionable business insights
- **Foundation-Building:** Create benchmarks for individual customer modeling

---

## 2 · Discovery Framework

### Cohort Dimension Discovery:
**Objective:** Identify which customer groupings reveal meaningful revenue patterns

**Exploration Process:**
1. **Data-Driven Exploration:** Analyze available customer attributes to identify potential grouping variables
2. **Business Context Integration:** Consider business operations, campaigns, and strategic decisions that might create distinct customer groups  
3. **Hypothesis Formation:** Generate testable hypotheses about which dimensions might show different revenue patterns
4. **Statistical Validation:** Test hypotheses using appropriate statistical methods

### Pattern Discovery Process:
**Objective:** Identify systematic revenue behaviors within and across cohorts

**Discovery Steps:**
1. **Visual Pattern Recognition:** Use exploratory data analysis to identify potential patterns
2. **Hypothesis Development:** Formulate specific hypotheses about observed patterns
3. **Model Testing:** Test different mathematical representations of patterns
4. **Business Validation:** Confirm patterns align with business understanding and external factors

---

## 3 · Implementation Phases

### Phase 1: Data Foundation and Exploration
**Prerequisites:**
- Historical transaction data with customer identifiers
- Customer acquisition and interaction data
- Business context documentation

**Process:**
- Comprehensive data quality assessment
- Initial exploratory analysis to identify potential cohort dimensions
- Hypothesis generation based on data patterns and business context
- Cohort definition and validation

**Success Criteria:**
- Clean, validated dataset ready for analysis
- Initial hypotheses about meaningful cohort dimensions
- Cohort definitions with adequate sample sizes
- Business stakeholder alignment on exploration approach

### Phase 2: Pattern Discovery and Validation
**Prerequisites:**
- Phase 1 completed with defined cohorts
- Hypothesis framework established
- Analysis infrastructure ready

**Process:**
- Systematic testing of revenue pattern hypotheses
- Model fitting and validation using hypothesis-driven approach
- Cross-cohort comparative analysis
- Business driver analysis and external validation

**Success Criteria:**
- Validated revenue patterns with statistical significance
- Clear understanding of pattern drivers and business context
- Reproducible analysis framework
- Stakeholder-validated insights and recommendations

---

## 4 · Analysis Methodology

### Hypothesis-Driven Pattern Discovery:
**Process:** Formulate specific, testable hypotheses about revenue patterns, then systematically validate or refute them using appropriate analytical methods.

### Pattern Validation Framework:
**Statistical Testing:** Use appropriate tests to validate pattern significance
**Cross-Validation:** Ensure patterns hold across different time periods
**Business Logic Check:** Confirm patterns align with business understanding
**External Validation:** Link patterns to known business events and market factors

### Interpretability Requirements:
**Business Translation:** Every pattern must be explainable in business terms
**Visual Communication:** Clear, annotated visualizations for all findings
**Decision Support:** Connect insights to specific business decisions and actions
